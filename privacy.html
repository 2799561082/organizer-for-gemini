Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Organizer for Gemini ("the extension", "we") is a Chrome extension that adds folders, bulk delete and export to gemini.google.com. It is built to keep your data on your device. This policy explains what data the extension handles.

Data that stays on your device

  • Folder names, folder assignments and cached chat titles are stored in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local). They are not synced or uploaded.
  • Exports (Markdown, ZIP, PDF) are generated inside your browser and saved directly to your downloads folder.
  • Bulk delete works by operating Gemini's own on-page controls in your tab. The extension does not read your Google cookies or tokens and does not call Google APIs.

The only data sent to our server

If you activate a Pro license, the license key and a random per-installation instance ID are sent to our license server, which verifies them with our payment provider, Creem (creem.io). This happens on activation, deactivation and about once a day for re-validation. No chat content, titles or browsing data is ever included.

Payments

Purchases are processed by Creem as merchant of record. Creem collects your email, billing and payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive your email address and order information to deliver your license key and provide support. We never see your full card details.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or remote code.
  • We don't sell or share your data. We never see your conversations.

Permissions

  • storage: save folders and license status locally.
  • alarms: schedule the daily license re-check.
  • Access to https://gemini.google.com/*: show folders in Gemini's sidebar and perform the exports and deletes you request.

This website

This website is static and does not use cookies or analytics. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (e.g. IP address) for security.

Removing data

Uninstalling the extension removes all locally stored data. To request deletion of purchase-related records, email us.

Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change accordingly.
