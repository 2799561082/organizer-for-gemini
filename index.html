FAQ

 

Is my chat data uploaded anywhere?

No. Folders and cached titles are stored locally in your browser (chrome.storage.local). Exports are generated in your browser. The only network request is license validation, which sends only your purchase email and a random installation ID.

How do I activate Pro?

Open the extension's Settings, enter the email address you used at checkout and click Activate.

Can I export to PDF?

Not yet. PDF export is coming soon for Pro. Today you can export any chat to Markdown (.md), or several chats as a .zip, on the free plan.

Can I cancel the monthly plan?

Yes, anytime. You keep Pro until the end of the paid period. Use the link in your receipt email or contact support.

What is your refund policy?

Full refund within 14 days of purchase, no questions asked. See the Refund Policy.

Is this an official Google product?

No. Organizer for Gemini is an independent extension, not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.

Which browsers are supported?

Google Chrome and most Chromium-based browsers (e.g. Edge, Brave) that support Chrome Web Store extensions.
